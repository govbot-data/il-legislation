HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, The United States Constitution requires an 
3enumeration of the population every ten years, with the 
4results of that enumeration serving as the basis for 
5apportionment of representation in the United States House of 
6Representatives; and
 
7    WHEREAS, The decennial census is one of the most 
8consequential constitutional functions of the federal 
9government, affecting congressional apportionment, federal and 
10state redistricting, and the allocation and administration of 
11governmental resources and funding; and
 
12    WHEREAS, The United States Census Bureau has operated 
13without a director confirmed by the United States Senate since 
14the resignation of Robert Santos in early 2025; and
 
15    WHEREAS, The Census Bureau is already making critical 
16decisions regarding the methodology, technology, testing, and 
17administration of the 2030 Census, making permanent and 
18accountable leadership at the bureau necessary to ensure an 
19accurate and reliable enumeration; and
 
20    WHEREAS, The Census Bureau's continued reliance on 
21differential privacy, statistical adjustments, and other

1methodologies that alter reported census data has raised 
2serious concerns regarding whether published data accurately 
3reflect the actual population of the United States and the 
4people residing within each state and locality; and
 
5    WHEREAS, The Census Bureau has acknowledged that its 
6disclosure avoidance system uses statistical techniques that 
7add or subtract noise from census data, and the bureau is 
8currently evaluating methodologies for the 2030 Census; and
 
9    WHEREAS, The use of statistical adjustments and other 
10methodologies that modify reported census data can diminish 
11the accuracy, transparency, and public confidence necessary 
12for census information to serve its constitutional and 
13statutory purposes; and
 
14    WHEREAS, The Constitution's requirement that an 
15enumeration be conducted every ten years demands an accurate 
16accounting of the population and not the use of census data for 
17social or political engineering; and
 
18    WHEREAS, The removal of a citizenship question from the 
19decennial census questionnaire has prevented the census form 
20itself from providing a direct accounting of the citizenship 
21status of persons included in the census population count; and

1    WHEREAS, The absence of accurate and comprehensive 
2citizenship data has contributed to concerns that the presence 
3of noncitizens within the population count may affect 
4congressional apportionment and state and federal 
5redistricting in ways that do not accurately reflect the 
6citizen population represented by elected officials; and
 
7    WHEREAS, Accurate citizenship information is necessary and 
8valuable to legislators, policymakers, courts, and the public 
9for lawful constitutional and statutory purposes; and
 
10    WHEREAS, The accuracy and reliability of the 2020 Census 
11remain matters of legitimate concern, and the federal 
12government should undertake a thorough review of the 
13methodologies, procedures, and results of the 2020 Census and 
14consider appropriate and lawful corrective measures where 
15deficiencies are identified; and
 
16    WHEREAS, The Census Bureau having a permanent director is 
17particularly important at this time because foundational 
18decisions affecting the 2030 Census are being made now and 
19will shape the nation's population count for years to come; 
20therefore, be it

22HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that

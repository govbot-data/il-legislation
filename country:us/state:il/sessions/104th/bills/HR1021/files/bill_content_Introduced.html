9    WHEREAS, Rev. Dr. Sampson was born in Everett, 
10Massachusetts on November 27, 1938; he graduated from Everett 
11High School in 1956, where he distinguished himself as a 
12gifted orator; he received a bachelor's degree in religion and 
13social science from Shaw University in Raleigh, North Carolina 
14in 1963; he continued his education in Illinois, earning a 
15Master of Cultural Studies from Governors State University in 
161973 and a Master of Divinity from McCormick Theological 
17Seminary in 1977; he later received a Doctor of Divinity from 
18Union Baptist Theological Seminary; and
 
19    WHEREAS, While at Shaw University, Rev. Dr. Sampson served 
20as student body president and held leadership roles in the 
21NAACP at the campus, city, and state levels; he participated 
22in the student sit-in movement in Raleigh, during which time 
23he was arrested for the cause of equal rights, and he helped

HOUSE RESOLUTION

 
2    WHEREAS, Mexican Americans have played an integral role in 
3the history, development, economy, culture, and civic life of 
4the State of Illinois, contributing through agriculture, 
5manufacturing, construction, transportation, business, 
6education, healthcare, public service, the arts, and service 
7in the United States Armed Forces; and
 
8    WHEREAS, Generations of Mexican American families have 
9established and strengthened communities throughout Illinois, 
10building neighborhoods, businesses, schools, churches, 
11cultural institutions, labor organizations, and community 
12organizations that have helped shape the character and 
13prosperity of our State; and
 
14    WHEREAS, Mexican Americans have enriched Illinois while 
15maintaining deep connections to Mexican history, traditions, 
16language, family, and culture, demonstrating that Mexican 
17heritage and American identity are complementary parts of the 
18Mexican American experience; and
 
19    WHEREAS, Recognizing Mexican American history and 
20contributions provides an opportunity to preserve this 
21heritage, strengthen civic participation, and inspire future 
22generations; and

1    WHEREAS, August 10 falls exactly 36 days after July 4, the 
2Independence Day of the United States, and 36 days before 
3September 16, the Independence Day of Mexico, symbolizing the 
4bridge of history, culture, and shared identity represented by 
5Mexican Americans; and
 
6    WHEREAS, The Mexican American Day initiative was conceived 
7and developed by the North American Institute for Mexican 
8Advancement (NAIMA), and it was first officially recognized by 
9the Village of Melrose Park, establishing Illinois as the 
10birthplace of this observance; and
 
11    WHEREAS, Mexican American Day was launched with the 
12broader vision of encouraging communities throughout the 
13United States to recognize, document, preserve, and celebrate 
14the history and enduring contributions of Mexican Americans; 
15therefore, be it
 
16    RESOLVED, BY THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ONE 
17HUNDRED FOURTH GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS, that 
18we declare August 10 as Mexican American Day in the State of 
19Illinois in celebration of the history, identity, 
20achievements, and enduring contributions of Mexican Americans 
21to our State and nation; and be it further

1    RESOLVED, That we encourage residents, educational 
2institutions, community organizations, businesses, and public 
3officials throughout Illinois to observe Mexican American Day 
4by recognizing Mexican American history and contributions and
